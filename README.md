# GMP Calculator: Calculate IPO Listing Price and Expected Listing Gain

Investing in IPOs requires proper research and an understanding of market trends. A [GMP Calculator](https://www.finowings.com/financial-calculators/gmp-calculator/) helps investors estimate the possible listing price and potential listing gain of an IPO using its issue price and Grey Market Premium (GMP). It makes calculations simple and helps investors understand the possible returns before an IPO gets listed on the stock exchange.

## What Is a GMP Calculator?

A GMP Calculator is an online financial tool used to estimate the potential listing price of an IPO. GMP stands for Grey Market Premium, which represents the unofficial premium at which IPO shares are traded before their official listing.

By entering the IPO issue price and current GMP, investors can calculate the estimated listing price and listing gain percentage within seconds.

## How to Calculate IPO Listing Gain?

The IPO listing gain can be calculated using a simple formula:

**Estimated Listing Price = Issue Price + GMP**

**Listing Gain (%) = (GMP ÷ Issue Price) × 100**

For example, if an IPO issue price is ₹500 and its GMP is ₹100, the estimated listing price becomes ₹600. The potential listing gain is 20%.

## Does GMP Guarantee IPO Listing Gains?

No, GMP does not guarantee actual IPO listing performance. It is based on unofficial market activity and investor sentiment. Actual listing prices can be different due to market conditions, company performance, subscription demand and other factors.

## Conclusion

A GMP Calculator is a useful tool for estimating IPO listing prices and potential gains. However, investors should not depend entirely on Grey Market Premium. Analysing company fundamentals, financial performance and market conditions is equally important before making investment decisions.
